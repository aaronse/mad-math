# Security Policy

Please report suspected security issues privately.

- Email: aza@makergalaxy.com
- Do not open public issues, pull requests, or discussions for unpatched vulnerabilities.

When reporting an issue, include:

- the affected file, feature, or build target
- the commit, version, or environment where you reproduced it
- reproduction steps or a minimal proof of concept
- the expected impact and any suggested mitigations

We will review reports privately and coordinate remediation and public disclosure when appropriate.
